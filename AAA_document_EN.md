## §2. Methodology: What Makes an Analogy Rigorous

### 2.1 Four Classes of Analogies

**Metaphor** — similarity of images in language without structural transfer. «Time is money.»
No predictive force. The domains converge on the surface of language, not at the level
of logic.

**Model analogy** — transfer of structural relations between elements from source domain
to target domain. M. Hesse (1966): an analogy is rigorous when positive features
(demonstrated structural similarity) outweigh neutral ones, and negative features
(known differences) are explicitly acknowledged and bounded.

**Structural analogy** — formal isomorphism: the same mathematical or logical structure
governs both domains. Here the analogy becomes proof, since both phenomena obey one law.

**Archetypal analogy** — transfers structures of meaning (narrative, cultural, aesthetic),
not structures of mathematical relations. This fourth class is not present in Hesse's (1966)
taxonomy. It is introduced here as an original extension of her framework, required to account
for cultural and narrative sources of architectural principles in BeTrueCore.
Its rigor requires separate consideration — see §2.2.

### 2.2 The Archetypal Analogy and Three Criteria of Rigor

Introducing the fourth class requires a test: does the archetypal analogy satisfy the same
three criteria as a scientific one?

**Relational criterion:** the archetype transfers a structure of roles and functions —
witness, path, trial, return. This is a structure of semantic relations, not mathematical ones.
The criterion is met in the semantic, not the formal, sense.

**Productive criterion:** the archetype generates design principles that could not have been
articulated without it. «Notary, not judge» — a direct consequence of the image of
Dede Qorqud. «Isolation as the condition of genuine choice, not its limitation» — a direct
consequence of the image of Odysseus at the mast. The criterion is met.

**Limiting criterion:** the archetype sets meaning and purpose, but not algorithm. The
narrative does not specify code. This is explicitly acknowledged. The criterion is met.

**Conclusion:** the archetypal analogy is rigorous in the semantic, not the formal, sense.
This does not make it weaker than a scientific analogy — it operates at a different level.
A scientific analogy defines the mechanism. An archetypal analogy defines the goal and
principle. Both levels are necessary: a system without a mechanism is a utopia;
a system without a purpose is an instrument without direction.

### 2.3 Three Criteria of Rigor for All Classes

The three criteria below are derived from Hesse's (1966) framework of positive, neutral,
and negative analogy, and reformulated for application to architectural analogies.
The names differ; the logical content is preserved.

**Relational:** is the structure of relations between elements preserved, not surface resemblance?

**Productive:** does the analogy generate new hypotheses, or only explain the already known?

**Limiting:** are the limits of the analogy explicitly named? Honest acknowledgment of
known differences is a mark of rigor, not weakness.

Every analogy in §3 and §4 is examined according to the scheme:
*Source → Target → What is transferred → Productivity → Limits → Class.*

## §3. Precedents: Analogy as an Instrument of Discovery

Scientists turned to analogies from other fields of knowledge to make complex abstract
concepts tangible, to overcome intuitive barriers, or to build a bridge between the known
and the new.

**Boltzmann and Maxwell — billiard balls and the theory of gases (19th century)**
Source: classical mechanics (a system of colliding elastic bodies).
Target: thermodynamics and statistical physics.
What is transferred: not the image of balls, but the mathematical apparatus — probabilistic
velocity distribution, conservation of momentum in collision, statistical averaging across
an ensemble.
Productivity: the laws of thermodynamics were derived from Newtonian mechanics; the
foundations of statistical mechanics were established as an independent discipline.
Limits: molecules are not rigid balls; quantum effects and nonlinearity in real gases
require going beyond the analogy. The authors themselves acknowledged this explicitly.
Class: structural analogy.

**Gamow — linguistics and the genetic code (1954)**
Source: linguistics and information theory (alphabet, words, translation between codes).
Target: molecular genetics.
What is transferred: the structure of coding — a finite alphabet, combinatorics of triplets,
a mechanism for translating one code into another.
Productivity: enabled the formulation of the triplet code hypothesis before its experimental
decipherment; created the language of bioinformatics (transcription, translation, code reading).
Limits: language is created with intention; DNA is the result of blind evolution. The
semantic analogy does not hold — only the syntactic one.
Class: structural analogy.

**Prigogine — thermodynamics and self-organization (Nobel Prize, 1977)**
Source: thermodynamics of open systems.
Target: theory of living organisms and social systems.
What is transferred: the principle — a system maintains internal order only through a
continuous input of external energy and the release of entropy into the environment.
A closed system tends toward disorder; an open one can accumulate complexity.
Productivity: explained life as a physically lawful phenomenon; introduced the concept of
ordered structures emerging from chaos (dissipative structures).
Limits: energy exchange does not explain consciousness, meaning, or subjectivity.
Class: structural analogy.

**Adam Smith — physical forces and market coordination (1776)**
Source: mechanics (self-regulating equilibrium systems).
Target: classical political economy.
What is transferred: the structure — distributed participants without a central coordinator
produce collective order through local interactions under shared rules.
Productivity: explained the phenomenon of spontaneous market order; opened the path to
game theory and coordination mechanisms.
Limits: market participants are not physical bodies; markets are not protected from
monopoly and information asymmetry.
Class: model analogy.

## §4. Analogy in BeTrueCore: How Principles Are Born from Analogies

### 4.1 The Principle of the Sovereign Signal

*Source analogy: the teacher / student relationship.*

In genuine learning, the teacher does not deposit knowledge — the teacher creates conditions
for the student to discover it themselves. This happens only in the absence of the pressure
of external evaluation.

This structure is transferred to BeTrueCore and gives rise to the principle
*«Secret citizen, transparent decision»*, which solves two problems simultaneously.

**First: self-knowledge.** Each participant sees only their own trajectory — movement
relative to themselves over time. Without comparison to others. Without an observer.

**Second: a clean collective signal.** When each person makes a genuine personal choice
in cryptographic silence — their sum produces an uncontaminated collective signal.

> *The more sovereign each person is in their decision — the cleaner the collective decision.
> This is not a paradox. This is an architectural principle.*

It is precisely the simultaneous resolution of both problems that makes the architecture
unique. The analogy provided the structure. Cryptography provided the mechanism of its realization.

Limits: the teacher/student relationship is a dialogue between two people. BeTrueCore is
a protocol for thousands of anonymous participants. The analogy operates at the level of
principle, not mechanism.
Class: model analogy.

### 4.2 The Principle of Critical Control Points: HACCP and Thermodynamics → TDSH

*Source analogy 1: HACCP — the safety control methodology developed by NASA and
Pillsbury in the 1960s.*

HACCP rests on a simple logic: rather than reacting to contamination in the finished
product, identify in advance the Critical Control Points — stages of the process where
contamination can enter — establish acceptable limits, and monitor continuously.

The TDSH parameters were not derived from literature — the principle was transferred
from professional practice of HACCP, applied also in ISO-certified food production:
each process has its own set of critical control points with defined thresholds.
In BeTrueCore, the parameters were defined specifically for collective judgment.

What is transferred: the structure of the methodology. Collective judgment is also a
process with stages (formation → expression → aggregation of results). At each stage,
«contaminants» can enter: social pressure, manipulation, cognitive bias.
The 32 TDSH parameters are 32 Critical Control Points of the collective judgment process.
TDSH thresholds are the acceptable limits. The matrix 23×32 = 736 points is the continuous
monitoring system. The RED/YELLOW/GREEN traffic light system is the corrective action
triggered when a threshold is breached.

*Source analogy 2: Target Discharge Superheat (TDS, 32°C) —
a thermodynamic parameter in HVAC and refrigeration engineering.*

Superheat at the compressor outlet serves as a threshold parameter: its deviation signals
system degradation. The 32°C calibration point is the base reference in many systems.
From this comes the number **32** in the «32 TDSH parameters»: each parameter is
a «superheat» of collective judgment — deviation from its threshold signals degradation.

HACCP provided the methodology. Thermodynamics provided the number. BeTrueCore created
an instrument that existed in neither source domain.

Limits: HACCP works with physical contaminants of measurable concentration. The degradation
of collective judgment is partly measurable, partly subjective. TDSH approximates — it does
not reproduce HACCP precisely.
Class: structural analogy (HACCP → TDSH) + model analogy (TDS → 32 parameters).

### 4.3 The Principle of Isolation: Odysseus at the Mast → ZK-Proof

*Source analogy: Homer's Odyssey, the episode with the Sirens.*

Odysseus knew he would not withstand the Sirens' song if he heard it freely. He did not
eliminate the danger. He created an architectural condition: he ordered himself bound to the
mast. His genuine will survived — not through willpower, but through isolation.

What is transferred: the structure — a deliberate architectural constraint as the condition
of genuine choice. A ZK-Proof (Zero-Knowledge Proof) does the same thing mathematically:
the participant forms their decision in cryptographic silence. External pressure physically
cannot penetrate the moment of choice formation.

The difference between analogy and identity: the mast is a physical constraint for one
person. ZK is a mathematical construction for thousands of anonymous participants
simultaneously. The structure is the same: isolation as the condition of genuine will,
not its limitation.

Limits: Odysseus knew about the danger and consciously accepted the constraint. A
BeTrueCore participant need not understand cryptography — the protocol creates the
condition of isolation.
Class: archetypal analogy.

*Philosophical parallel: J. Krishnamurti — «The observer is the observed»
(Freedom from the Known, 1969). When the external observer is architecturally absent,
the observed signal is clean. This is the direct philosophical statement of the same
principle — and where the mathematical claim of Gödel's model analogy finds
its clearest intuitive ground.*

### 4.4 The Principle of the Witness: Dede Qorqud → Notary at L2 and L5

*Source analogy: the Turkic epic «The Book of My Grandfather Korkut».*

Dede Qorqud does not invent names — he reads the deed and names what already is.
He does not judge, does not govern — he only bears witness and passes it forward.
«Varlıq özü İmzadır» — existence itself is the signature.

What is transferred: the structure of witness without power over content. This archetype
is embodied at two points in the architecture.

L2 (Optimism): raw data is translated into objective, immutable identifiers. The protocol
reads the deed (verified choice) and records it — without interpretation, without adding
meaning.

L5 (AI agents): nine agents in read-only mode observe, record, and measure. They never
decide. «AI is the notary. The human is the author.» — constitutional principle in
IBeTrueCore.sol.

The analogy distinguishes observation from governance as architecturally distinct functions.
This cannot be articulated through technical terms with the same clarity.

Limits: Dede Qorqud is a living witness with cultural memory and judgment. L5 agents are
algorithmic systems without subjectivity. The archetype sets the principle, not the mechanism.
Class: archetypal analogy.
